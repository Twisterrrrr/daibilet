# Yaroslavl image source notes

Updated 2026-11-02. Corrected images use dated asset variants in `apps/web/src/lib/city-place-images-data.ts`. Each replacement has 1536x1024 main, 960x640 card, and 480x320 thumbnail derivatives. The existing undated files were also replaced to prevent stale defective art from being reused.

- `damanskiy-ostrov-r20261102`: adapted and cropped from [Leninskiy rayon, Yaroslavl, panoramio (225).jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Leninskiy_rayon,_Yaroslavl%27,_Yaroslavskaya_oblast%27,_Russia_-_panoramio_(225).jpg), by Алексей Петров, CC BY 3.0. It shows the actual amusement park on Damansky Island.
- `karabikha-r20261102`: adapted and cropped from [Большой дом с востока.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:%D0%91%D0%BE%D0%BB%D1%8C%D1%88%D0%BE%D0%B9_%D0%B4%D0%BE%D0%BC_%D1%81_%D0%B2%D0%BE%D1%81%D1%82%D0%BE%D0%BA%D0%B0.jpg), by Чуринъ, CC BY-SA 4.0. It shows the main manor house at the Nekrasov estate.
- `karabikha-vinnyy-sklad-r20261102`: adapted and cropped from [Бывший Винный склад.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:%D0%91%D1%8B%D0%B2%D1%88%D0%B8%D0%B9_%D0%92%D0%B8%D0%BD%D0%BD%D1%8B%D0%B9_%D1%81%D0%BA%D0%BB%D0%B0%D0%B4.jpg), by Чуринъ, CC BY-SA 4.0. It shows the actual brick remains of the historic wine store; the accompanying place description was updated to identify the ruins.
